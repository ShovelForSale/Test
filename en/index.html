      About us

      From jumbo roll to finished product

      Paper Converting makes V-fold and Z-fold hand towels, toilet paper, kitchen rolls and napkins. We convert jumbo rolls of paper into finished products: we cut, fold, rewind and pack them.

      We supply office buildings, hotels, restaurants, gyms and healthcare facilities with everything for hygiene:

      • Dispensers and refills: hand towels, soap, foam soap, toilet paper, kitchen rolls, bins and air fresheners
      • Kimberly-Clark products: Scott, Kleenex, KleenGuard, WypAll and Kimtech
      • Napkins and wet wipes printed with your company logo
      • Cleaning products, personal protective equipment and hotel amenities
